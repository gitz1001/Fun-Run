Welcome

Thank you for joining the Southville Run For A Cause 2026 — a community fun run organized by Southville for Others (SFO) and the Alumni Department.

This event brings together the entire SGEN community — students, parents, faculty, alumni, friends, and partners — for a day of wellness, unity, and giving back.

All proceeds will support the Pawikan Conservation Center in Bataan and the Bindoy Community in Dumaguete, helping learners with special needs.

Where your registration goes

Every peso raised supports these two communities.

Pawikan Conservation Center Bataan

Pawikan Conservation Center — is committed to protecting sea turtles through rescue, rehabilitation, research, and education. By safeguarding nesting sites and promoting marine conservation, the center helps preserve these endangered species. Every registration supports sea turtle care, conservation efforts, and educational programs, helping protect marine life and inspire environmental stewardship..

Bindoy Community Dumaguete

Bindoy Community — Your support helps SFO build a mini library for the Bindoy community, creating a safe and inspiring space where children can learn, read, and grow. Every registration brings us closer to expanding access to education and empowering the next generation through literacy.

Proceeds help learners with special needs in both communities.

Why do we run?

Southville Fun Run for a Cause!

We run for more than just the finish line. We run to make a difference, bring people together, and support a cause that matters. The Southville Fun Run for a Cause is a celebration of health, community, and compassion. Every step we take is a step toward helping others and creating a positive impact within our community.

Whether you run, walk, or simply show your support, every stride counts. Together, we turn our energy into purpose and our miles into meaningful change.

We run for a healthier community. We run for a greater cause. We run because every step can make a difference.
